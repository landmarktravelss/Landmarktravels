Board your overnight flight to Tokyo, where timeless tradition meets futuristic innovation. Get ready to trade your everyday surroundings for neon-lit streets, peaceful shrines, and unforgettable culinary experiences. Sit back, relax, and let the excitement build your journey to Japan begins now.

Konnichiwa, Tokyo! Arrive in the morning and meet your Tour Director and fellow travelers before transferring to your accommodation. After check-in, enjoy time to relax and settle in.

This evening, gather for a casual group meet-up to break the ice and go over the exciting days ahead. From this point on, our professional photographer will be capturing your best moments so you can fully enjoy the experience while we preserve the memories.
